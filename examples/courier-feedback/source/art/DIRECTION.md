# 라일락 우편배달부

A young adult trail courier in a quiet indie adventure: warm chestnut curls over a small, alert three-quarter face, a cream pointed collar and ochre scarf, a soft lilac jacket with turned creases, plum shorts, worn brown boots and a brass-fastened messenger satchel. The 96×112 transparent native-pixel cel uses a crown at (38,10), face center near (52,24), shoulders at y35, hips near (47,65), and ground boundary y100. The hair-inclusive head is 20px tall on a 90px figure, approximately 4.5 heads; the crown widens asymmetrically rather than forming a helmet. Highlights come from upper left, outlines soften into the local material color inside the figure, and shading uses compact pixel clusters. Garments and limbs are redrawn from full pose contours; no cutout rig, raster warping, masking or recycled rendered frames is used.

`ink.py` supplies only native drawing primitives and a small translated face/head drawing for identity continuity. `draw_lead.py` authors the full idle/hurt silhouette coordinates and can regenerate those state directories plus the master and contact sheets. Other action artists author their own full contours. `palette.json` is the shared color source; the `view` argument of `face()` currently documents the right-facing production contract only and does not mirror or transform the drawing.

Idle: open neutral, inhale/raised shoulder with longer scarf, blink, relaxed weight shift. Hurt: impact fold, deepest backward recoil with a raised far arm and split support, recovery, then a grounded settle with a protective hand at the chest. The face landmark remains attached to the authored neck position in every pose. Preview GIF backgrounds are diagnostic paper only; state PNGs remain transparent. Native/enlarged stills were inspected by the lead artist. Timed playback and final user art acceptance are separate checks.
